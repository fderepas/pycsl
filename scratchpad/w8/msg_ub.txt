fix(#43): widen UB-7.1's `_MUTATING_METHODS` — a fail-open that route #14 was accidentally masking

`IRScanner._MUTATING_METHODS` drives the UB-7.1 mutation-during-iteration detector, and it
omitted `sort`, `reverse`, `popitem` and the three set-difference updaters. So
`for x in xs: xs.sort()` was not flagged.

It never showed up as a live defect for a reason worth recording: route #14 refuses those
calls at EMISSION, before the detector is ever consulted. Measured directly — the `.sort()`
form died with the route-#14 diagnostic while the `.append()` form died with UB-7.1. That is
a HIDDEN COUPLING between two independent mechanisms, and retiring route #14 with the
length-carrying sequence capability would have silently reopened UB-7.1.

Widened in BOTH the live source and the mirror, so the detector stands on its own. Both
probes now hit UB-7.1.
