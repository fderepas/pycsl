gate(#43): NEW PLANE — bin/check-mirror-coverage.py, the one thing the headline number structurally cannot see

`bin/count-trusted-directives.py` counts `#@ \trusted` markers IN THE MIRROR. That answers
"how much of what the mirror models is assumed rather than proved". It cannot answer "how
much of the live emitter the mirror models AT ALL" — because a live function with no mirror
counterpart carries no marker. It is not trusted; it is ABSENT.

`bin/self-annotate-mirror-check.sh` walks the other direction (MIRROR-only defs). Nobody had
measured live-only defs.

FIRST MEASUREMENT: of 1820 live functions in the 53 files that HAVE a mirror, **550 (30.2%)
have no mirror counterpart** — concentrated in the big emitters (`expressions.py` 142,
`functions.py` 97, `statements.py` 92, `preamble.py` 71, `Module5_IREmitter.py` 35) — and a
further 41 live files have no mirror at all.

WHAT THIS IS AND IS NOT. It is NOT an unsoundness: an unmirrored function sits outside the
self-verification perimeter exactly like an unmirrored file. It IS a limit on what "456"
means, and a ratchet is the honest way to hold it — so that converting a `\trusted` stub
counts as progress and quietly adding an unmirrored live helper beside it does not.

Route #15's own Module 6 emitter `_emit_init_contract_checks` is precisely that case. It is
sound today — reachable only from `transpile` and `_emit_prefunctions_infra`, both `\trusted`
in the mirror, so the verified perimeter is unchanged in shape — and it is now COUNTED
rather than invisible. That is the point of writing the plane in the same window as the
change that motivated it.

Ratchets 550 / 41, negative-tested at 549.
