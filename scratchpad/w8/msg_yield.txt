audit(#43): yield-erasure's 2 resolved — unobservable today, and OBSERVABLE the moment `_Unparser.__init__` is converted

`_Unparser.block` and `.delimit` are `@contextmanager` generators consumed at 24
`with self.block():` / `with self.delimit(...):` sites. Route #20 established that
`_py_stmt_with` never reads `stmt.items`, so the manager call is dropped in every one of
those callers; the emitted `_unparser__block` / `_unparser__delimit` exist and are never
applied.

TODAY THAT IS UNOBSERVABLE, for exactly the reason no-op candidate (c) was refuted: the
emitted record is `type _unparser = {  }` — EMPTY — so `_indent` and `_source` are not model
fields and no contract can reference them.

**BUT IT BECOMES OBSERVABLE THE MOMENT `_Unparser.__init__` IS CONVERTED.** That conversion
was recorded earlier this relaunch as the single largest model upgrade left in the mirror:
ONE marker, and the whole class moves from the opaque `_pyobj_state` attribute store to a
CONCRETE RECORD carrying `mutable _indent: int` and `mutable _source: array int`. At that
point the dropped indent/dedent tracking is a real, referenceable divergence in a file
proved at 3373 goals.

SO THE TWO BACKLOG ITEMS ARE COUPLED AND MUST MOVE TOGETHER. Converting
`_Unparser.__init__` requires a context-manager protocol model — or re-trusting the 24
callers — in the SAME increment. Otherwise it converts a currently-unobservable erasure into
an exploitable one, which is the opposite of what the conversion is for. Recorded in the
handoff as a hard precondition rather than a note.

That is the third coupling this relaunch has surfaced between two mechanisms that looked
independent (the others: route #14's refusal masking UB-7.1's fail-open, and route #20
moving `pure_ast`'s emission out from under an in-flight proof).
