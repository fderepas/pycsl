gate(#44): NEW PLANE — bin/check-swallowed-exceptions.py, and four recognizers catch `Exception` where they mean `_PVWBail`

THE HAZARD. The emitter is full of recognizers that DECLINE by returning
None, and a decline falls through to a coarser lowering. That is the
design. But a broad `except Exception:` that swallows turns an INTERNAL
ERROR into the same decline — so a bug inside a faithful recognizer
silently downgrades the emission to the generic path, and this campaign has
now shown twenty-eight times that a generic path can be an ERASING one.

WHAT IT MEASURES, and it is BEHAVIOURAL rather than static:
  STATIC   141 broad swallowing handlers (`except:` / `except Exception` /
           `except BaseException`, body only pass/continue/bare-return/
           return-of-a-constant, never re-raising) across 137 functions in
           `src/pycsl`.
  DYNAMIC  every exception CAUGHT AND NOT RE-RAISED inside `src/pycsl`
           during a real emission of all 53 mirrors, via `sys.monitoring`'s
           EXCEPTION_HANDLED event.

THE BASELINE IS NOT ZERO AND THAT IS THE FINDING. Eight recognizers in
`generic_fold.py` catch their own `_PVWBail` decline signal, and FOUR spell
it `except Exception:` — `recognize_pyval_flatten`,
`recognize_pyval_list_search`, `recognize_pyval_list_walker`,
`recognize_pyval_string_walker`. Those four swallow a TypeError, an
AttributeError or a KeyError from a genuine bug exactly as they swallow
their own bail. Tightening them to `except _PVWBail:` is a four-line
byte-inert change, left as a named item for the next window.

Everything else that fires is designed control flow, and each was read to
confirm it: CPython's own `NodeVisitor` deprecation shim in
`pure_ast.visit_Constant` (156302x), the parser's backtracking `_try`
combinator (1724x), `GeneratorExit` in two iterator helpers, and
`auto_trust._check_witness_vals` catching `SyntaxError` (7x) — that last one
is fail-closed, because a `by` witness violating a class invariant makes
Why3 REJECT the type declaration.

HOW MUCH THE REST OF THE BATTERY SEES — MEASURED, NOT ASSERTED, and it
corrects my own first draft of this message. Injecting a TypeError inside
