gate(#44): remove the ir-field dispatcher-block fallback — all ten of its hits were false positives, three refuted by end-to-end probes

It took the blind spot 25 -> 6 and produced ten new hits.
`SumExpr.base/lo/hi` are read in `expr_ghost_spec_ops`,
`RaiseStmt.exc_value` in `_STMT_IR_CTORS`, `ForallItemsExpr.key/val/map`
through `_csl_forall_items`; and `SliceExpr.lower`, `SetCompExpr.elt` and
`DictCompExpr.generators` were each probed END-TO-END with a contract false
of the program — a lower-bounded slice, a set comprehension with a
non-identity element, a dict comprehension over a generator — and all three
correctly FAIL.

The cause is structural: a `t == "<Kind>"` region is usually a GUARD inside
some other recognizer, not the lowering.

A baseline of ten false positives trains the reader to ignore the gate,
which is worse than the blind spot it closes. Removed, and recorded in the
script so nobody rebuilds it. The 25 unmatched classes are printed on every
run instead, as an honest limit.
