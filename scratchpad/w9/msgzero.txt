This is the window's recurring lesson in its most general form. A refusal
that never fires (#43), a probe that deletes nothing (#44), a pin that
cannot trip (#44), and now a plane aimed at the wrong directory: every one
of them is indistinguishable from success. A gate must be able to tell
"nothing is wrong" from "I looked at nothing", and that has to be a check
in the gate, not a habit in the operator.
