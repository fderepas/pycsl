The lever executors' 79 measured chains are merged in sec. 3.

Method (scratch only, nothing landed). Per stub: port the VERBATIM live body into a scratch copy of the mirror (`bin/port-one-mirror-body.py --convert [--replace]`), emit `--no-proof --keep-mlw` (the Why3 type-check included) on QUICK (`scratchpad/cv9/probe.sh`, one probe per heavy.sh call), read the blocker, then patch THAT blocker in the scratch copy and re-emit, cumulatively (`scratchpad/cv9/specs_data.py` -> `spec/<tag>.json`, `patch.py`). Each patch is labelled: **equivalent rewrite** (the same Python, a form the emitter takes; a live refactor of the `\trusted` twin could do it), **mirror retype** (the mirror def line / field / class constant gets the live or a faithful type; fidelity-permitted), or **stand-in** (a typed helper `_cv9_*` or a literal of the right type that SIMULATES the missing capability; never a claim of faithfulness). A chain stops at TC-OK, at an HONESTY-WALL (the remaining body would be input-blind / a facade), or at DEPTH-STOP (5 probes reached, or the next wall is a value-model change no source stand-in can simulate, or the refusal is not localisable from the stub). 380 emissions in `scratchpad/cv9/probe/` (results.tsv, `<tag>.src.py/.log/.mlw`). Machine-readable: `scratchpad/cv9/chains.tsv` (cv9 stubs), `scratchpad/cv9/merged.json` (all chains incl. lever executors).

## 6. Reading for the driver

1. **Breadth result.** All 166 in-scope stubs were walked to a verdict (sec. 0). 15 reach TC-OK somewhere on their chain, but 14 of those are facades or need an unbuilt capability to be honest. The census has exactly **one conversion candidate**: `GhostSpecOpsMixin._handle_mktuple_expr` (expr_ghost_spec_ops.py, sec. 1). Its single link is a comprehension -> loop rewrite, which can be done as a LIVE refactor of its `\trusted` twin (no emitter change). After that it is TC-OK and input-reading, at the same honesty level as the verified expression handlers. Cost: a whole-file proof of the expr_ghost_spec_ops mirror (small; cv8 estimated 0.24 h) plus the live-source refactor's corpus byte-diff.
2. **Two near misses, each one SMALL emitter defect away.** `_ContractParser.parse` (s031) and `_ContractParser._parse_inductive` (s039) are blocked by **cap-trusted-val-unit-return**: a `\trusted` stub with a `pass` facade body is emitted as a val returning `unit` unless its annotation is a scalar, and retyping the annotation (`Any`, `CSLNode`, `Optional[Tuple]`, `List[Tuple]`) does not help. Simulated with a typed stand-in:
   - s039 reaches TC-OK (partial honesty: its list fields are `any_array ()`).
   - s031 gets down to "the stand-in does not write `self.i`", which is exactly the effect the real val carries.
   - The same defect sits on the chains of s025, s026, s033 and s047.

   Fix size S, risk low: the declared return type of an unconstrained val is a typing correction, not a new law. It moves only the mirrors that hold such vals: Module1, Module2, Module3. Its sibling defect **cap-return-exn-nonint** is on 5 cv9 chains and 1 lvA chain. It is an early `return` of a set / str / tuple / dict / mutable record that raises the int-typed `Return` exception. It is also small, but its lowering lives in the VERIFIED stmt_control_flow twin, so it owes a hot-file whole-file proof (lvA spiked it, not landed). Neither defect completes a stub ALONE except s031. They are the cheapest "clear a link on many chains" items.
3. **Set cover is flat.** Read the top single capabilities in sec. 4A with care. They complete HONESTY-WALL stubs through EXTERNAL-world models (a subprocess model completes `_coqc_version`, `_lean_version` and `_why3_typecheck`). That family is the DO-NOT-CONVERT class: building a subprocess or filesystem model to convert CLI glue is not this campaign's business. In scope (sec. 4B), no single capability completes more than 2 closed stubs, and no 3-bundle completes more than a handful. The 3-bundle with the most stubs whose KNOWN walls are covered is three value-model sub-capabilities (irnode + dict + tuple/list). Those chains do not close, though: they are DEPTH-STOPs and may continue.
4. **The value-model seam is the campaign.** VMSEAM sits on most merged chains and is the terminal wall of most cv9 DEPTH-STOPs (sec. 5). Measured link by link, the sub-walls in order of stubs are:
   - **cap-vm-unerase-irnode**: Any-typed IR dicts read through `.get` / `[]`. Also: Module2's CSLNode classes vs the emit_ir ADT (a CSLNode value cannot flow where ExprIR is expected); emit_ir child fields like `Proj.index`; and pure_ast node fields typed two incompatible ways (record field = emit_ir, accessor = int).
   - **cap-vm-unerase-list**: List[str] locals bound from calls stay int even when annotated; `seq` (append-built) vs `array` (parameter) lists; enumerate over List[str].
   - **cap-vm-unerase-dict**: dict-of-list / dict-of-tuple values, len / iteration of a map field, JSON-record dicts.
   - **cap-vm-unerase-tuple / -union**: tuple returns and elements; Optional[...] dataclass fields and returns; the Optional-of-tuple truthiness test.

   Each is emitter-wide (every mirror + corpus re-emit). A mirror RETYPE of the stub's own signature is free and cleared 48 links. Two limits show up, though: (i) it often only MOVES the wall to a caller or callee that keeps the erased type (s022, s130: the heterogeneous `needs` dict must be unerased for the whole preamble mixin in one step); (ii) annotations on LOCALS are not honoured (s001, s118, s132).
5. **Free links.** 40 links were cleared by equivalent rewrites: tuple-unpack alias, nested unpack target, closure params made explicit, comprehension -> loop, a mutator on a fresh unaliased local -> rebinding, pop -> slice. These can be applied to the LIVE source of the `\trusted` twins with no emitter change, but they almost never complete a stub. They are worth bundling with a capability batch that removes the terminal wall of the same stubs, not on their own (lvE reached the same conclusion for #327).
6. **Stop probing the external-world stubs.** 40 HONESTY-WALL stubs and 8 TC-OK facades share one property: their only observable is a filesystem / subprocess / json / hashlib / argparse / stdout / eval effect. Their chains here are the evidence for moving them to DO-NOT-CONVERT. `audit_proof*`, the pycsl.py CLI layer (`_run_proofs`, `_dispatch_provers`, `_run_vacuity_gate`, `main`, `_run_audit_mode`, ...), `ir_resolve`'s FS resolution and `_process_dependency`.
7. **Recommended order.**
   1. Convert `_handle_mktuple_expr` (live refactor + one small whole-file proof).
   2. A DEFECT batch: cap-trusted-val-unit-return now; cap-return-exn-nonint when a stmt_control_flow proof slot is available. Then re-probe s031, s039, s025, s038, s141 and s143 with this census's specs (`scratchpad/cv9/specs_data.py` reproduces every patched chain).
   3. Plan the value-model work as a multi-batch track, ordered irnode -> list -> dict -> tuple/union. The per-stub chains in sec. 2 list, for each stub, which sub-wall it hits after the previous one clears.
